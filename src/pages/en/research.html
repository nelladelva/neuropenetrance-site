Methods in plain words

Simple by design

Each of six genes is compared with healthy controls in the same study; the score is the three inflammation genes minus the three lymphocyte genes. No fitted weights. The probability is calibrated across studies, and every study's result is reported separately.

Results

Reported in full

AUC 0.61 on 7 studies the signature had never seen, above chance in 8 of 8, about the same as a standard blood count. Read the full evidence →

Code & app

Open to inspect

The app's code is public on GitHub, and the app is free for academic research. The analysis code is available on request for collaborations and replication.

Ways to collaborate

Research collaborations we're looking for

  • Joint studies on blood immune profiles in depression and related conditions
  • Sharing a cohort, especially with CRP, cytokines or treatment-response data, to test the inflammatory subgroup
  • Student projects and theses in data science, neuroscience or psychiatry
  • Using the app in your own research, with our support

Research updates: what we're testing next

Whether the inflammatory subgroup matches measured CRP; whether it predicts treatment response; and whether plasma proteins (proteomics) sharpen the signal.
