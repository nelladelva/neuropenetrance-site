What we do for partners

From blood RNA to decisions you can act on

We work with whole-blood gene expression (RNA), the kind you already collect in PAXgene or Tempus tubes.

🧪

Analyse your samples

Send blood RNA data from your cohort. We score every patient on the immune signature and report what separates them.

🎯

Stratify your trial

Identify the inflammatory subgroup (about 1 in 5 patients in our data) to enrich or stratify recruitment.

📈

Model treatment response

With your trial or follow-up data, we build and test predictors of response and disease development, tested on held-out data.
