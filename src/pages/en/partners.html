Stratify your trial

Identify the inflammatory subgroup (about 1 in 5 patients in our data) before or during a trial, to enrich recruitment or analyse response by subgroup.

📈

Model treatment response

With your trial or follow-up data, we build and test predictors of treatment response and disease development, always checked on data held back from model building.

How a partnership works

Four clear steps

  1. Intro callYour question, your samples, your timelines. No data shared yet.
  2. Data-sharing agreementScope, data protection (GDPR) and ownership of results, in writing.
  3. AnalysisQuality checks, scoring, subgroup and response analyses, with honest performance estimates.
  4. Report & next stepsA written report, a results call, and options for a follow-up study.
What to send

What we need from you

  • Whole-blood RNA data: RNA-seq counts, microarray data or a qPCR panel (PAXgene or Tempus tubes are ideal)
  • Basic labels: patient or control, and age and sex if available
  • For prediction: follow-up outcomes or treatment response
  • Pseudonymised data only; we never need names

Typical timeline: a first analysis of an existing dataset within 4–6 weeks of signing the data-sharing agreement.
