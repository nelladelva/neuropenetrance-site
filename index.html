Penetrance profiling for neurodevelopmental disorders

Sequencing tells you who's at risk. It doesn't tell you who gets sick.

NeuroPenetrance turns a genetic result into a functional prediction — using patient-derived neurons and machine learning to show which variant carriers actually go on to develop a neurodevelopmental or psychiatric disorder.
